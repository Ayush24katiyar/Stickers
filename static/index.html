Paste a link from X, Tenor, Giphy or anywhere — GIFs, images and full videos all work. Videos are squeezed into a 512 × 512 transparent sticker that fits WhatsApp's limits (≤ 10 s, ≤ 500 KB); pick any 10 seconds of the clip.

Fine print — what sticker can and can’t do
  • WhatsApp decides the specs: 512 × 512 px · still ≤ 100 KB · animated ≤ 500 KB · max 10 seconds · frames ≥ 8 ms (we use 10 ms).
  • Anything longer is cut to the 10-second window you pick — GIFs and videos alike. Un-trimmed GIFs keep the first 10 s.
  • Video stickers are silent. WhatsApp allows no audio in stickers, and this tool drops it.
  • Still images become single-frame stickers. Very busy 10-second clips get re-compressed harder — lower quality, then fewer frames — to reach 500 KB.
  • Uploads: GIF, PNG, JPEG, WebP ≤ 20 MB · videos up to 250 MB. Links: https only · ≤ 20 MB (≤ 40 MB for video links) · private / localhost links are refused.
  • X / Twitter: public posts only, first media only, and its MP4 usually needs a moment to load.
  • Importing: WhatsApp has no API to add a sticker directly — open the .webp in a sticker-maker app (Sticker Maker, Sticker.ly, …) to use it.
